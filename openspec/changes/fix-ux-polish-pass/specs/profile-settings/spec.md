## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Help & FAQ entry navigates to real screen
The profile screen's "Help & FAQ" entry SHALL navigate to a functional Help & FAQ screen, not a stub or toast.

#### Scenario: User taps Help & FAQ
- **WHEN** the user taps "Help & FAQ" on the profile screen
- **THEN** the app SHALL push the Help & FAQ screen with renderable content

### Requirement: Privacy Policy entry navigates to real screen
The profile screen's "Privacy Policy" entry SHALL navigate to a functional Privacy Policy screen.

#### Scenario: User taps Privacy Policy
- **WHEN** the user taps "Privacy Policy" on the profile screen
- **THEN** the app SHALL push the Privacy Policy screen with renderable content

### Requirement: Contact MLA Office entry navigates to real screen
The profile screen's "Contact MLA Office" entry SHALL navigate to a functional Contact MLA Office screen.

#### Scenario: User taps Contact MLA Office
- **WHEN** the user taps "Contact MLA Office" on the profile screen
- **THEN** the app SHALL push the Contact MLA Office screen with MLA office contact details
