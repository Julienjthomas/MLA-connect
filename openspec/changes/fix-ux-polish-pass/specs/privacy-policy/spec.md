## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Privacy Policy screen displays policy content
The Privacy Policy screen SHALL display the app's privacy policy as scrollable text with a "Last updated" date footer.

#### Scenario: User opens Privacy Policy
- **WHEN** the user navigates to Privacy Policy
- **THEN** the policy text SHALL render in full
- **THEN** a "Last updated" date SHALL be visible at the bottom
- **THEN** the screen SHALL have an app bar with a title and back navigation
